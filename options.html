Popup Hotkeys for SteamDB

Hover over a game on steamdb.info, then press a shortcut.

Keyboard shortcuts

    Click a shortcut, then press the new key. Esc cancels, Backspace turns it off.

    Store page

    About

    Adds keyboard shortcuts to the Store, Wishlist, Follow and Ignore buttons in the game popup on steamdb.info, so you don't have to chase the popup with your mouse.

    Made by razingo. Source code, bug reports and ideas: github.com/razingo/hotkeys-steamdb.
